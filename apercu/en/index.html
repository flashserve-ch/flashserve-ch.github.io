Frequently asked questions

What to know before joining the pilot program.

Am I committed when I book?

Not for the pre-booking: it is free and without commitment. If you then join the pilot, the partner subscription at CHF 59.90 per month is a firm 12-month commitment starting January 1, 2027. We present the detailed terms to you before anything is signed.

What equipment do I need?

Almost nothing. We install a few small, discreet devices in your venue, included in the partner subscription. Your team follows orders in real time on an online dashboard, from a computer or a tablet. Your customers order from their smartphone. Setup takes 1 to 2 weeks: site audit, menu configuration, a 2-hour training for your team, then launch.

Does FlashServe work with my point-of-sale system?

FlashServe works alongside your current point-of-sale system, without replacing it. During the pilot, orders arrive on the FlashServe dashboard. A direct connection with point-of-sale software is planned for a later version.

What happens to my customers' data?

Data protection is built in from the start. Only the information needed for the order is collected, session data is deleted as soon as the order is closed (unless the customer agrees to a loyalty program) and everything is encrypted. Customers give their consent at every session and can delete their data in the app. FlashServe complies with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and the EU GDPR.

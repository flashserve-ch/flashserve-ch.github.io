We sell time.

At peak hours, every minute of waiting weighs on your service. FlashServe gives that time back to your team and your customers.

  • Queues cost sales

    At peak hours, a long line discourages customers before they even order.

  • Overloaded staff

    During the rush, your team juggles taking orders, payments and service at the same time.

  • Customers who leave

    When the wait is too long, customers walk away, and don't always come back.

How it works

Three steps. Zero queue.

  1. 1

    1. Arrive

    The customer's phone detects the venue automatically.

    BLE + geofencing
  2. 2

    2. Order

    The menu opens on their phone and they place their order.

    Right from their seat
  3. 3

    3. Get served

    The order reaches the team instantly.

    In real time
